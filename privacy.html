Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Swingle Sports Analytics operates Side or Serve ("we"), a beach volleyball scoring and analytics service at sideorservevb.com. This policy explains what we collect, who can see it, and your choices. We do not sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, name and sign-in method (Google, or email and password). Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
  • Profile: your name, team or club, role (manager or coach), an optional program ID, distance units and any custom venues you add.
  • Match data you enter: player and opponent names, scores, who served, side switches, venue, court, competition level, wind conditions, and the statistics calculated from them.
  • Subscription details: status, trial and renewal dates, card brand and last four digits, and Lemon Squeezy customer and subscription IDs. Lemon Squeezy collects and stores your payment details; we never see your full card number.
  • Location: only when you tap Detect My Location. It is used to find the nearest venue, and coordinates are saved only if you create a custom venue.
  • Weather lookups: we ask OpenWeather for conditions at a venue's coordinates. No personal information is sent.
  • Technical logs: our hosting providers keep standard logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and reliability.

How we use it

To run the app, calculate your statistics and recommendations, process subscriptions, provide support, keep the service secure, and understand overall usage through anonymous totals.

Who can see your data

  • You, and the people you give access to. A linked partner sees the matches you share with them. A scorekeeper you invite can keep score for you and see your stats and matches, but cannot delete anything or change your profile. You can remove either at any time.
  • Coaches in the same program. Coaches who use the same program ID can see the live status of the program's in-progress matches.
  • The public, only if a scorer chooses. A match appears on the public live scores page only when its scorer turns that on, and it shows team names and scores.
  • Anonymous totals. Venue and community numbers, such as which side scores more, are counts and percentages with no names attached.
  • Our service providers, who process data for us: Google Firebase (sign-in and database), Netlify (hosting), Lemon Squeezy (payments) and OpenWeather (weather). Our team can also access the database to operate the service and give support.
  • When the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile at any time.
  • Deleting a match removes it from your history and your statistics. Copies shared with a partner remain with them.
  • To delete your account and data, or to ask for a copy, email brian@gasavb.com. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Cancel your subscription from the manage subscription link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email.

Children

Side or Serve is for people 13 and older. Players under 18 should use it with a parent or guardian, who can ask us to delete the account.

Security and changes

Data is encrypted in transit and protected by access rules so users can read only their own records and those shared with them. No system is perfectly secure. We will post changes to this policy here with a new date, and tell you about material changes.
